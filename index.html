Shipping Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

WHERE WE SHIP

Checkout shows the destinations and shipping options currently available. Our fulfillment partners may ship orders from different locations, and items in one order can sometimes arrive separately.

SHIPPING COSTS AND DELIVERY ESTIMATES

You will see shipping charges and any delivery estimate before you pay. Delivery estimates are our best guide, but carrier, customs, weather, and supplier delays can affect arrival. Any duties or import charges not collected at checkout may be payable under your destination's rules.

PREPARATION AND TRACKING

We start preparing your order after payment is confirmed. Once it is handed to the carrier, we will share tracking when it is available. Tracking updates may take a little time to appear. Please check your email and delivery address at checkout. If you spot an address mistake, contact us quickly; changes may not be possible once preparation or dispatch has started.

DELAYS

If we learn that your order will miss the delivery time shown at checkout, we will contact you with an update and next steps. If a delay means you are entitled to cancel for a refund under the rules that apply to your order, we will explain how to do that.

CANCELLATIONS AND REFUNDS

If you need to cancel, contact us as soon as possible. We can request cancellation before dispatch; it is confirmed once our supplier approves it. After dispatch, cancellation under our standard policy is unavailable. If an item arrives damaged or there is another delivery problem, please email us so we can help. Customer protections that apply where you live continue to apply. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method; processing time depends on the payment provider.

DELIVERY QUESTIONS

Email desck.co.admin@gmail.com with your order number and a short description of the issue. We aim to reply within 2 days and will help you understand the available next steps.
